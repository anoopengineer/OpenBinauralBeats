How binaural beats work

Each ear hears a slightly different pure tone, say 200 Hz on the left and 240 Hz on the right. Your brain combines them and you perceive a soft pulse at the difference: 40 Hz. That pulse is the binaural beat.

Because the effect happens between your ears, you need stereo headphones. Speakers mix the two tones in the air before they reach you.

Frequently asked questions

What are binaural beats?

Binaural beats happen when each ear hears a slightly different tone, for example 200 Hz in the left ear and 240 Hz in the right. Your brain blends the two and perceives a soft pulse at the difference, in this case 40 Hz. Many people use that pulse to help them focus, relax, meditate or fall asleep.

Do I need headphones?

Yes. Each ear has to hear its own tone, so you need stereo headphones or earbuds. Speakers mix the two tones in the air before they reach you, and the effect disappears.

What are 40 Hz binaural beats?

40 Hz is in the gamma range, which is linked to attention and concentration and is the most studied binaural beat frequency. The 40 Hz Focus preset plays 200 Hz in one ear and 240 Hz in the other, which makes a good background for deep work.

Which binaural beat frequency is best for sleep?

Delta beats, from 0.5 to 4 Hz, are associated with deep sleep, and theta beats, from 4 to 8 Hz, with drowsiness and meditation. Try the Deep Sleep preset (2 Hz) with the session timer, so the sound fades out on its own.

Is OpenBinauralBeats really free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, no account, no subscription and no in-app purchases. The source code is on GitHub.

Does it collect any data?

No. The app makes no network connections at all. Your settings and saved presets stay in a small file on your computer.

Do binaural beats actually work?

The evidence is mixed. Some studies show modest effects on attention, anxiety and relaxation, and the brain does respond measurably to the beats. Effects vary a lot between people. Treat it as a focus and relaxation aid, not a treatment.

What volume should I use?

Lower than you think. The beat works at a quiet, comfortable level. Start around 20 to 30 percent and adjust from there.

Are binaural beats safe?

For most people, yes. Don't use them while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. If you have epilepsy, a seizure disorder or another neurological condition, check with a doctor first. Stop if you get a headache or feel uncomfortable.
